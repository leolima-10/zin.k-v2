# brag-plan.md — zin.k startup portfolio brag video

## Angle & Hook

**What is it (one sentence):** zin.k is a startup that builds custom websites and digital solutions for enterprises — from institutional sites to internal systems — with no templates, design and code integrated, and results-focused delivery.

**Who it's for and what it does:** For companies that need a custom digital presence that loads fast, positions the brand, and converts visitors into leads or customers — without cookie-cutter templates or endless revisions.

**What sets it apart:** 
- **No templates** — every project is born from zero, from briefing to delivery
- **Design and code in the same room** — what's approved in Figma is exactly what ships
- **Result, not decoration** — performance, SEO and accessibility come from factory
- **4 weeks from briefing to site** — predictable, no surprises

**Most impressive claim:** "Dois projetos por mês, do briefing ao ar — mantendo qualidade e atenção ao detalhe."

**Visual hook:** The zin.k logo + headline appearing with the tagline, followed by the 4-step process animation, and the "aceitando novos projetos" badge.

## Tone

**`default`** — punchy, playful, clean. Fits a startup portfolio that wants to be taken seriously but not take itself too seriously.

**Pacing / transitions:** 4–5 scenes; soft transitions. Not as fast as `chaotic`, not as restrained as `polished`.

## Scenes & Durations

Target: **18 seconds** (sweet spot per creative laws)

| Scene | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 2.5s | Logo + headline "sites e soluções digitais sob medida" + tagline fade-in. "aceitando novos projetos" badge pulses. |
| **Reveal** | 3s | 4-step process visualization: briefing → design → desenvolvimento → entrega & suporte. Line draws connecting steps. |
| **Highlight 1** | 3s | "Sem template" — custom design flow, Figma to code pipeline. Figma frame mockup animating to live site. |
| **Highlight 2** | 3s | "Resultado, não enfeite" — Performance counters (Pagespeed 98+, 4 weeks), speed lines, before/after subtle hint. |
| **Highlight 3** | 3s | "Design e código na mesma mesa" — developer/designer collaboration visual, the integrated workflow. |
| **Punchline/Outro** | 4s | "Vamos criar juntos?" CTA with WhatsApp button. Final frame holds the settled state. |

**Total:** 18.5s (rounds to ~19s, within 15–25s range)
